Biceps tendon: active modifications

  • Neutral (hammer) grip on all loaded exercises. No underhand grip until pain-free
  • Avoid pull-ups, underhand rows, hammer curls and heavy deadlifts
  • Stop any exercise that reproduces forearm rotation pain
  • Triceps alternatives: rope pushdowns, overhead rope extensions, cross-body cable extensions
